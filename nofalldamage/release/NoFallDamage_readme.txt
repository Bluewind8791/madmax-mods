NoFallDamage 1.0.0 for Mad Max (Steam)

Max takes no damage from falls and does not die from very high falls.
All other damage (enemies, bullets, explosions, cars hitting Max) works as normal.
Other characters still take fall damage.

Requirements
- Ultimate ASI Loader (x64) installed as dinput8.dll in the game folder.
- Steam version of Mad Max. On another game version the mod stays inactive and writes
  "not supported" to nofalldamage.log.

Install
- Vortex: add this archive and enable it.
- Manual: copy nofalldamage.asi into the game folder (next to MadMax.exe).

Tested
- Falls from low and very high places: no health loss, no death.
- Enemy and car damage: unchanged.

Uninstall
- Disable the mod in Vortex, or delete nofalldamage.asi and nofalldamage.log.
